## Quick usage guide & main features

### 1. What can you do?

- Visually create professional Discord embed messages.
- Instantly switch light/dark theme and language (Spanish/English).
- Manage as many webhooks as you want—saved locally in your browser.
- Save templates, recover and share them via unique links. Bookmark frequent templates in your browser for instant access.
- Live preview while you edit.
- All confirmations and alerts appear as elegant modals (never annoying popups).
- Optional donation banner and always-visible heart button to support development.
- All legal and privacy info easily accessible in the footer.

---

### 2. How to use each feature

- Fill out your embed fields in the sidebar—live preview updates in real time.
- In “Webhooks”, add your Discord webhook URLs. Switch quickly between several.
- Click “Send Message” when ready and your bot will receive the message in the selected channel.
- To reuse a layout, click “Save as template.” Access all saved templates from the templates menu.
- To share your design, use the button to copy the unique template link and share it with anyone.
- If you use a template often, bookmark its URL in your browser favourites for one-click access.
- Your language, theme and webhooks/templates are stored ONLY in your own browser.
- Review and accept the privacy/cookie policy when you first visit, or any time from the footer.
- To support the project, use the daily banner or the heart icon at the top-left.

---

### 3. Tips to make the most of the app

- Bookmark the URLs of your favourite templates for instant access, no searching.
- Don’t worry about losing anything: all is saved locally; not even the creator can access your data.
- Change language and theme at any time—they’re always remembered.
- The donation banner is optional and does not affect any feature.
- For questions, just open a GitHub Issue.
